Questions

How do I play?
Choose a mode, then drag your finger anywhere on the screen. The ring follows your finger. Catch dots of the color shown at the top; avoid other colors and the gray cross. The full rules are on the About page.
There is no sound.
The game respects the Ring/Silent switch so it never interrupts your music. Turn off Silent mode and check that sound is on in the game’s settings.
I find some colors hard to tell apart.
Turn on “Color symbols” in the game’s settings: each color gets its own shape. It is on automatically if iOS Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Differentiate Without Color is enabled.
The screen flashes too much.
Turn on iOS Settings → Accessibility → Motion → Reduce Motion. The game then skips screen flashes and pulsing.
My score is not on the leaderboard.
Leaderboards need Game Center. Sign in at iOS Settings → Game Center, then finish a round. The game itself works without Game Center.
What happens when I leave the game mid-round?
The round is paused. Come back and tap “Continue”, or go to the menu.
How do I reset my best scores?
Best scores are stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets them. Game Center leaderboard entries are managed by Apple.
Do you collect my data?
No. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
Is there an Android version?
Not yet. iPhone comes first.
