Catch the Color

A fast arcade game for iPhone. Drag the ring with your finger and catch only the dots of the right color. The color keeps changing, the dots keep getting faster.

Original title: «Лови цвет». No ads, no accounts, no data collected. Works offline.

Help & contact

How to play

Move your finger anywhere on the screen and the ring follows it, a little above your fingertip so you can always see it. The color you need is shown at the top of the screen.

  • Catch dots of the current color. A wrong color costs one of your three lives.
  • +3White dot: +3 seconds on the clock.
  • ♥Pink heart: gives back a lost life.
  • ✕Gray cross: never catch it, it costs a life.

Every 5 correct catches in a row raise the score multiplier, up to ×4. One mistake resets it.

For players who find colors hard to tell apart, the app can draw a distinct symbol on each color. It turns on automatically with the iOS setting “Differentiate Without Color”.

Two modes

Classic · 60 seconds

Score as much as you can before the timer runs out.

No Time Limit

No timer. The round lasts until you lose all three lives, and the dots keep speeding up.

Best scores are kept for each mode. If you are signed in to Game Center, you can compare them with other players on the leaderboards.

Privacy in one line

The app collects no personal data, has no ads, analytics or tracking, and asks for no permissions. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
