Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how the iPhone game Catch the Color («Лови цвет», the “App”) handles information. The App is developed and operated by Ruslan Zaripov (“we”, “us”).

  • We do not collect, store or sell any personal data.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The App asks for no permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.
  • Best scores and settings stay on your device.

Information stored on your device

The App keeps your best score for each game mode and your settings (sound, vibration, color symbols) in the device’s local storage. This information never leaves your device through the App and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you delete the App.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the App submits your best scores to the App’s Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is a service provided by Apple: your score and Game Center nickname are processed by Apple and shown to other players according to your Game Center settings and Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive your Apple ID, email or any contact information. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in iOS Settings → Game Center; the App keeps working without it.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The App contains no analytics, advertising or attribution SDKs and makes no network requests of its own. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), build profiles or track you across apps and websites.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and aggregated usage statistics. They do not identify you, and we use them only to fix bugs.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Support emails

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply to you, and delete the correspondence on request.

Changes to this policy

If the App ever starts handling data differently, we will update this page before releasing that version and change the date at the top.
